OSM has 121.6 of the City's 124.0 km of on-road lane on the right side of the road, and the buffer on 18.0 of the 22.7 km where the City records one. Of what the City installed since 2025, 2.4 of 10.5 km is not in OSM yet. 2.9 km of City lane is drawn on the road centreline, where its side cannot be read: those are judged on any side, and their rows say so.
