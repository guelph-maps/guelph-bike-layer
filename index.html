Guelph Bike Facilities

A reference layer of the cycling network published by the City of Guelph — 1,514 lines from the City's Bike Facilities layer, 210.2 km of them in service — coloured by what is on the ground, ready to add to the OpenStreetMap editors iD and JOSM while you map.

Two layers — which to use

Vector layer Interactive. Click a line to read its tags (layer bike: id, status, type, kind, onroad, buffer, year, width in m, signed, street, length in m). Zooms 14–19. Best in iD.
Raster layer Lines drawn directly on the tiles, coloured as above. A backdrop over aerial imagery, from z14. Best in JOSM.

Add to iD

Vector layer (interactive)

  1. Open the iD editor and press U for the Map Data panel.
  2. Under Custom Map Data, open the settings and paste this URL:
https://guelph-maps.github.io/guelph-bike-layer/tiles/vector/{z}/{x}/{y}.pbf

Zoom in, then click any line to see its tags in the inspector.

Raster layer

  1. Open the Background panel and choose Custom.
  2. Paste this URL template:
https://guelph-maps.github.io/guelph-bike-layer/tiles/raster/{z}/{x}/{y}.png

As a background it replaces the imagery; the vector layer is the one to use over imagery in iD.

Add to JOSM

Raster layer (recommended for JOSM)

  1. Open Preferences → Imagery and click +TMS.
  2. Paste this entry (the tms[14,17] prefix sets the zoom range; JOSM scales it up past z17):
tms[14,17]:https://guelph-maps.github.io/guelph-bike-layer/tiles/raster/{zoom}/{x}/{y}.png

Give it a name such as Guelph Bike Facilities, click OK, then enable it from the Imagery menu above your aerial imagery. Note JOSM's placeholder is {zoom}, not {z}.

Vector layer (optional)

JOSM can also add the vector tiles via Preferences → Imagery → +MVT: 

https://guelph-maps.github.io/guelph-bike-layer/tiles/vector/{zoom}/{x}/{y}.pbf

Tagging

Painted lane cycleway:left|right=lane on the road, the side relative to the way's direction. Green paint: cycleway:<side>:surface:colour=green.
Buffered lane … plus cycleway:<side>:buffer=yes, or :separation=rumble_strip.
Separated cycleway:<side>=track with :separation=kerb|bollard, or a separate highway=cycleway.
Multi-use path highway=path + bicycle=designated + foot=designated + segregated=no.
Signed route A member of a route=bicycle relation (network=lcn); the road itself carries no lane.

Notes

  • The City's lane lines are drawn 4–10 m either side of the road centreline, so the side a lane is on can be read off the overlay. A few kilometres are drawn on the centreline itself; check the side on imagery.
  • Adding a lane or a buffer at a time from imagery or survey, with this layer as a guide, is ordinary mapping. Copying the City's lines or tags into OSM by script would be an import, and needs an import proposal and community discussion first.
  • INSTALLYEAR is the City's own install year. Rows dated this year or last are what the gap page calls new.
  • Vector tiles are pre-rendered through zoom 19; raster tiles for z14–17 (the editor overzooms them further).
  • Tiles are refreshed weekly from the City's service.
  • This is a reference layer — check it against current imagery before copying anything into OpenStreetMap. Where a way already exists, improve it in place rather than replacing it, so its history and tags survive.
